Process

Four steps, zero surprises

The same process on every engagement, scaled to the size of the brief. You always know what happens next, what it costs and who owns it.

I work in fixed weekly cycles with a demo at the end of each one, a written decision log and a price you approve before the work starts. No scope drift, no surprise invoices, no handover gaps between the person who designed it and the person who shipped it.

01

Discover

Stakeholder interviews, analytics review and a map of the jobs your customers need done. We agree the success metrics before anything is drawn.

  • 1 week
  • Research
  • Metrics
02

Design

Flows, wireframes and high-fidelity screens with a clickable prototype, plus the design system that keeps it consistent at scale.

  • 2-4 weeks
  • Figma
  • Prototype
03

Build

Accessible front-end plus the back-end your product needs, shipped in weekly increments you can click and criticise.

  • 2-8 weeks
  • React
  • PHP, Node
04

Launch & learn

Performance and accessibility audits, launch photography, analytics wiring and a handover your own team can run with.

  • 1 week
  • QA
  • Handover
