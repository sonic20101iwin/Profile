Product & UI/UX design that engineers enjoy building

Most products do not fail because the screens are ugly. They fail because nobody owns the whole story: the research, the flows, the system behind the screens and the code that ships it. I take that ownership from the first workshop to the production deploy, so decisions are made once instead of three times.

Every engagement starts with a short discovery sprint: stakeholder interviews, analytics review and a map of the jobs your customers are really trying to do. That becomes a set of flows, wireframes and a clickable prototype you can put in front of real users within two weeks.

What is included

  • Discovery workshops, user research and success metrics agreed up front.
  • Flows, wireframes and high-fidelity interface design in Figma.
  • A token-based design system with documented components, states and motion.
  • Accessibility review to WCAG 2.2 AA, including keyboard and screen-reader paths.
  • Hand-over sessions with your engineers, plus a living style guide they can extend.

How it runs

Design happens in one-week cycles with a demo at the end of each. You get a shared board, a written decision log and a fixed price per cycle, so the scope never drifts quietly. Typical product design engagements run six to fourteen weeks.

Engagement options

Fixed-scope projects for defined outcomes, sprint-based work when you need momentum, or a monthly retainer when you want a senior partner on call. Hourly consulting starts at $180; retainers are quoted after the intro call.
