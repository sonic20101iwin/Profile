Pulse Banking

  1. Home
  2. Portfolio
  3. Pulse Banking

A dashboard customers actually finished using

Pulse Banking came to me with a product that investors loved and customers abandoned: a nine-step onboarding flow, a dashboard nobody could read at a glance and a front-end that three agencies had patched in turn. I joined as one accountable owner for research, design and front-end.

What I did

  • Ran 18 usability sessions and rebuilt the information architecture around the four jobs customers actually do.
  • Designed a token-based design system: colour, type, spacing, 62 components, documented for engineers.
  • Built the production front-end in React and TypeScript with WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard paths throughout.
  • Shot the launch photography for the marketing site, press kit and out-of-home campaign.

The outcome

Activation rose 38% within two sprints of launch, support tickets about the dashboard fell by roughly half, and the design system cut front-end hand-off time by 40% across four product lines. The client has since extended the engagement twice.
